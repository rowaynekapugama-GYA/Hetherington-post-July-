Feature · NSW Minerals Week
An evening of genuine hospitality
NSW Minerals Week began with Beneath the Surface, Hetherington's first-ever client appreciation evening. Held at the Teahouse, Crown Sydney, guests enjoyed curated food and beverage experiences before a fireside conversation with two-time world champion boxer Billy Dib, who brought real perspective, honesty and impact to the room.
A chance to pause, connect and celebrate the relationships that sit at the core of what we do — beyond the work, beyond the titles, and beneath the surface.
Thank you to our valued clients, supporters and industry peers who joined us and made the evening what it was.

Recent regulatory updates
The rules are moving in four jurisdictions
What our consultants are tracking, and what it means for your tenements, timelines and compliance obligations.
NSW
Streamlining native title assessment: the Infrastructure Facility Process
The Harvey High Court ruling replaces the Right to Negotiate for ancillary mining activities — halving the objection window to two months.
SA
Reforms propose to extend the life of exploration licences
Holders could apply for unlimited two-year "special circumstances" extensions beyond the current 18-year limit.
NT
Mineral titles framework modernised — Amendment Act now in force
Renewal periods extended from two to six years, new small-scale and fossicking titles, and simpler preliminary exploration consents.
WA
Review of the State Petroleum Legislative Framework
Five "use it or lose it" reform proposals, from a shorter commerciality window to stronger powers over inactive production licences.
NSW
Community consultation code now fully enforceable
New Consultation Plan, engagement contact and annual reporting obligations apply from 31 March 2026.
