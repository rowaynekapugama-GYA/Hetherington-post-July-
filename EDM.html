NSW Minerals Week · Feature
Beneath the Surface
NSW Minerals Week began with our first-ever client appreciation evening at the Teahouse, Crown Sydney — curated food and beverage experiences followed by a fireside conversation with two-time world champion boxer Billy Dib, who brought real perspective, honesty and impact to the room. A chance to pause, connect and celebrate the relationships at the core of what we do: beyond the work, beyond the titles, and beneath the surface.

What's happening at Hetherington
Exploration & Tenures Forum — our 10th year as main sponsor
David Salim opened the NSW Minerals Council's Exploration & Tenures Forum 2026 at the InterContinental Sydney, with Assistant Manager Jemima Moffat presenting land access case studies in the first session. The day closed with a guest address from the Hon Courtney Houssos MLC — a strong way to start the week.

Cultural heritage and native title in focus at AMEC
Hetherington sponsored AMEC's Cultural Heritage to Native Title Forum in Perth, with David Salim giving the opening address on where the landscape is heading post the Kelly Review, and Madelaine Cannell-Lunn joining the conversation. We also hosted an AMEC Boardroom Lunch at the Melbourne Hotel, joined by Hon Matthew Swinbourn MLC.

Investing in the next generation of leaders
Hetherington is proudly sponsoring Karinne Moss and Emily-Rose Clemence in the 2026 NSW Women in Mining mentoring program — and congratulations are due twice over, with both recently promoted to Senior Consultants in our Sydney office.
